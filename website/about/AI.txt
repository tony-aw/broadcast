
# No AI

No AI was used in creating/developing the 'broadcast' package.
I avoid the use of AI out of principle due to the following reasons:

 - It makes the maintenance of the code dependent on AI.
 - (unintended) theft of intellectual property is a serious risk when using AI [@mueller2024; @cooper2025].
 - some AI models are, at least in their initial training phases, trained with underpaid (child) labour in developing countries [@muldoon2025] . I don't support that.
 - training of - and even inference with - generative AI models consumes a considerable amount of fossil-based electricity and even a lot of water, which is not great for the environment [@DeVries2026; @bozkurt2025].
